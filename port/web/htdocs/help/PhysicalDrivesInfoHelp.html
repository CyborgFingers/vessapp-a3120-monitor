Physical Drives

The Physical Drives–Information tab provides information about the physical disk drives installed in the subsystem enclosure, including:

To view information about the physical drives in this enclosure:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Enclosures icon.
  3. Click on the Enclosure icon.
  4. Click on the Physical Drives icon.

Physical Drive Information

  • Device – PD plus the slot number where the drive is installed.
  • Model – Drive Manufacturer's model name and/or number.
  • Type – SATA.
  • Configurable Capacity – Configurable Capacity of this physical drive in GB.
  • Location – Enclosure number and Slot number.
  • Operational Status – OK is normal. Can also show Rebuilding, Forced Offline, or Dead.
  • Configuration Status – The array to which the drive is assigned or its spare designation.

To view more information about a specific physical drive, click the Device ID link.

Locate a Physical Drive in the Enclosure

To locate a physical drive in the enclosure:

Click on the physical drive you want to locate in the Enclosure Front View graphic.
The disk status LED will flash to identify the carrier holding the drive.
