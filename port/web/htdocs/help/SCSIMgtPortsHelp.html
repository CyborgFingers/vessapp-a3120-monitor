SCSI Channels

The SCSI Management–Channels tab enables you to see the current Data Channel settings, including:

  • Channel ID – 1 or 2
  • Max Transfer Rate – 320 MB/s is the default
  • Termination Status – Auto, On or Off
  • Target ID List – The active target ID number

To access SCSI Channel settings, click on Channel 1 or Channel 2.

To access this tab:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree view.
  2. Click on the Administration Tools icon.
  3. Click on the SCSI Management icon.
  4. Click the Channels tab in Management View.
