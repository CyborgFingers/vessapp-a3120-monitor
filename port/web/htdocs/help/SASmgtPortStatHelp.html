SAS Port Statistics

The SAS Managment Statistics tab displays statistical information about all of the SAS ports on the subystem.

To view information about the SAS ports:

  1. Click the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click the Administrative Tools icon.
  3. Click the SAS Management icon.
  4. In the Management window, click the Statistic tab.
    The statistics for the selected port appear the screen.

To change port settings, see Port Settings.

To clear statistics, see Clearing Statistics.
