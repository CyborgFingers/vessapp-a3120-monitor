iSCSI Ping

The iSCSI Management–Ping tab enables you to verify a network connection through the subsystem's iSCSI data ports.

To send a ping:

  1. Click the Subsystem icon in Tree view.
  2. Click the Administration Tools icon.
  3. Click the iSCSI Management icon.
  4. Click the Ping tab in Management View.
  5. Enter the IP address you want to ping in the IP Address field.
  6. Choose an iSCSI data port from the Ping Through Port dropdown menu.
    • : port 1 or 2
    • : port 1, 2, 3, or 4
  7. Click the Submit button.
    After a few moments, the result displays in the iSCSI Ping Operation header.
