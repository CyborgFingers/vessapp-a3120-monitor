Spare Drive

The Spare Drive–Information tab provides information about the selected spare disk drive.

When a physical drive in a disk array fails and a spare drive of adequate capacity is available, the disk array will begin to rebuild automatically using the spare drive.

  • Spare ID – Consecutive number assigned when the spare drive was created.
  • Physical Drive ID – The ID number of this physical drive.
  • Spare Type – Global, can be used by any disk array. Dedicated, can only be used by the assigned disk array.
  • Revertible Spare – Yes or No. A revertible spare drive automatically returns to its spare drive assignment after the failed physical drive in the disk array is replaced. See Transition.
  • Operational Status – OK is normal. Can also show Rebuilding or Offline.
  • Spare Check Status – The result of the Spare Check function. Healthy, Failed or Not Checked.
  • Physical Capacity – Theoretical capacity of the drive.
  • Configurable Capacity – Capacity of the drive actually available for use.
  • Block Size – Sector size in bytes.
  • Drive Interface – SATA.
  • Model – Drive Manufacturer's model name and/or number.
  • Location – The enclosure and slot number where the spare drive is physically installed.
  • Configuration Status – Type of spare drive: Global, Revertible Global, Dedicated, Revertible Dedicated.
  • Operational Status – OK is normal. Can also show Rebuilding or Offline.
  • Serial Number – From the drive manufacturer.
  • Firmware Version – From the drive manufacturer.

To locate a spare drive in the subsystem enclosure:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Enclosures icon.
  3. Click on the Enclosure icon.
  4. Click on the Spare Drives icon.
  5. Click on the Spare Drive icon.
  6. In Management View, click on the spare drive in the Enclosure Front View graphic.
    The disk status LED will flash to identify the carrier holding the drive.

To change the parameters of a spare drive, click the Settings tab.
