Yellow !s and Red Xs

displays yellow exclamations points (!) and red Xs in Tree View to identify components that need attention.

When a yellow exclamation point (!) appears over a Subsystem, follow the exclamations to the lowest component with the red X. This is the source of the malfunction.

Click on the component with the red X and look in the Management Window for more information.

Also see Logical Drive Degraded and Troubleshooting.
