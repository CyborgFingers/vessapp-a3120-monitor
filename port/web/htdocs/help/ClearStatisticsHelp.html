Clear Statistics

Clear Statistics is a function that clears statistical data on controllers, physical drives and logical drives.

To clear statistical data:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree view.
  2. Click on the Administration Tools icon.
  3. In Management View, click on the Clear Statistics link.
    The Clear Statistics tab appears in Management View.
  4. Click on the Submit button.
  5. In the confirmation box, type the word confirm in the field provided.
  6. Click the OK button.
