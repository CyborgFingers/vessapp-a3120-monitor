Network Management VLAN

The Network Management–VLAN tab enables you to see the current list of VLAN entries.

To view the current VLAN entries:

  1. Click the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click the Administrative Tools icon.
  3. Click the Network Management icon.
  4. Click on the VLAN tab in Management View.

VLAN entry information consists of:

  • VLAN Tag number
  • Member Interface
  • MTU – Regular, 1500 B. With jumbo frames enabled, 9000 B
  • IP Address – IP address of the VLAN
  • SubMask – Subnet Mask of the VLAN

To add a VLAN, click on the VLAN tab dropdown menu and choose Create VLAN.

To change the settings of a VLAN, click the VLan Tag link of the VLAN you want to change.

To delete a VLAN, click on the VLAN tab dropdown menu and choose Delete VLAN.
