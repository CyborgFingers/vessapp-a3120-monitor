Transition

Transition is the process of replacing a revertible spare drive that is currently part of a disk array with an unconfigured physical drive or a non-revertible spare. The revertible spare drive returns to its original status. In order to run the Transition function, the spare drive must be Revertible. For more information on Transition, see Chapter 7 of the Product Manual.

In addition, you must specify an unconfigured physical drive of the same or larger capacity to replace the revertible spare drive.

Run a Transition

To run Transition, take the following actions:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. From the dropdown menu on the Background Activities tab, choose Start Transition.
  3. From the Source Physical Drive dropdown menu, choose the disk array and revertible spare drive.
    The disk array is called an Array. The revertible spare drive is called a Seq No.
  4. From the Target Physical Drive dropdown menu, choose an unconfigured physical drive.
  5. Click on the Submit button.

The revertible spare drive will be listed under the Spare Drives icon and the disk array's opertional status will show OK.

Pause, Resume, or Stop the Transition

To pause, resume, or stop the Transition:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Disk Arrays icon.
  3. Click on the Disk Array icon.
  4. Click on the Background Activities tab in Management view.
  5. Under Background Activities, click on the Transition link.

Note: You can also pause, resume, or stop the Transition from the Subsystem icon Background Activities tab.

Set Transition Priority

To set Transition priority, take the following actions:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. From the dropdown menu on the Background Activities tab, choose Settings.
  3. Click on the Transition Rate dropdown menu and select a rate: Low, Medium or High.
    • Low allocates fewer system resources to the Transition Rate and more to data read/write operations.
    • Medium allocates a balance of system resources to the Transition Rate and data read/write operations.
    • High allocates more system resources to the Transition Rate and fewer to data read/write operations.
  4. Click on the Submit button.
