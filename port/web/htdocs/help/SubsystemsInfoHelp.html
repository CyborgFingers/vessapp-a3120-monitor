Subsystems

The Subsystems–Information tab lists subsystem you are logged into, plus the other subsystems on your network. Each individual subsystem is identified in the Tree by its Management Port IP address.

To access the Subsystems–Information tab, click on the Subsystems icon in Tree View. There are no settings on this tab.

Show All Subsystems

There are no user settings on this tab.

To see all of the subsystems on your network:

  1. Move your cursor over Storage Network in the Header.
    A "Show Network Subsystems" message will appear.
  2. Click on the "Show Network Subsystems" message to display the subsystems.
    The list will show all subsystems on the network at the time the GUI was launched.
  3. To update the subsystem list, click the Discover button at the bottom of the list.

To log into any of the displayed subsystems:

  1. In Tree View, click on the Subsystem icon of the subsystem you want to see.
    If your username and password do not match the subsystem you are logging into, the log in screen will appear.
  2. Log in to the new subsytem, as needed.
