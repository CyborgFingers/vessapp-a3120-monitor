Logical Drive Settings

The Logical Drive–Settings tab enables you to assign an alias and make cache settings to this logical drive.

To make Logical Drive settings:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Disk Arrays icon.
  3. Click on the Disk Array icon.
  4. Click on the Logical Drives icon.
  5. Click on the Logical Drive icon.
  6. Click on the Settings tab in Management View.
  7. Enter a name into the Alias field.
    Maximum of 32 characters. Use letters, numbers, space between words and underscore.
  8. From the Read Policy dropdown menu, select a Read Cache policy.
    The choices are Read Cache, Read Ahead and No Cache.
  9. From the Write Policy dropdown menu, select a Write Cache policy.
    The choices are Write Back and Write Through (Thru). If you select No Read Cache, Write policy is automatically Write Through.
  10. Click the Submit button.
