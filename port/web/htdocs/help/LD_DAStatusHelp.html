Logical Drive / Disk Array Status

Status refers to the operating condition of a logical drive or disk array. You should be familiar with these various conditions so you can understand what is happening to your logical drive or disk array and whether you need to take action.

  • OK – This is the normal state of a logical drive or disk array. When a logical drive or disk array is OK, it is ready for immediate use. For RAID Levels other than RAID 0 (Striping), the logical drive has full redundancy.
  • Synchronizing – This condition is temporary. Synchronizing is a maintenance function that verifies the integrity of data and redundancy in the logical drive or disk array. When an logical drive or disk array is Synchronizing, it will function and your data is available. However, access will be slower due to the synchronizing operation.
  • Rebuilding – This condition is temporary. When a physical drive has been replaced, the logical drive or disk array automatically begins rebuilding in order to restore redundancy (fault tolerance). When a logical drive or disk array is rebuilding, it will function and your data is available. However, access will be slower due to the rebuilding operation.
  • Incomplete – A physical drive missing from the disk array. You can accept the disk array as is or replace the missing physical drive. If you accept the disk array as is, your logical drives might be offline.
  • Degraded – This condition arises as the result of a physical drive failure. A Degraded logical drive or disk array will still function and your data is still available. However, the logical drive or disk array has lost redundancy (fault tolerance). You must determine the cause of the problem and correct it.
  • Offline – This condition arises as the result of a second physical drive failure. An Offline logical drive or disk array is not accessable but some or all of your data may remain intact. You must determine the cause of the problem and correct it.
  • Transport Ready – After you perform a successful Prepare for Transport operation, this condition means you can remove the physical drives of this disk array and move them to another enclosure or different drive slots. After you relocate the physical drives, the disk array operational status will show OK.

To check the status of your Logical Drives:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Logical Drives Summary icon.
  3. Look in the Operational Status column in the Management Window.

To check the status of your Disk Arrays:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Disk Arrays icon.
  3. Look in the Operational Status column Management Window.

Also see Logical Drive Degraded and Logical Drive Offline.
