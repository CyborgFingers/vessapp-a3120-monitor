SCSI Targets

The SCSI Management–Targets tab enables you to see the current settings for the data targets.

To access to the SCSI Management–Targets tab:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree view.
  2. Click on the Administration Tools icon.
  3. Click on the SCSI Management icon.
  4. Click the Targets tab in Management View.

Information:

  • Channel ID – 1 or 2
  • Target ID – 0 through 15
  • Status – Enabled or Disabled
  • Negotiated Transfer Rate – Data Transfer Rate in MB/s
  • Bus Width – Narrow or Wide

A value of N/A under Transfer Rate or Bus Width appears when the Target is disabled.

To enable SCSI Targets, click on the Channel tab, then click on Channel 1 or Channel 2.
