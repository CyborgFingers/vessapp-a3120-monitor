Controllers

The Controllers–Information tab provides information about the controllers in a subsystem. Controller information includes:

  • Controller ID (1 or 2)
  • Alias, if assigned
  • Status
  • Model
  • Operational Status – OK is normal. Missing means not present or offline.
  • Readiness Status – Active or Standby.

  • Locate – Click on the button to locate the controller. See below.

To locate the controller in the subsystem:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Controllers icon.
  3. Click on the Locate Controller button for the controller you want.
    The Controller Location LEDs (on the back of the subsystem) will flash for one minute.

For more information about an individual controller, click on the Controller 1 link.

Note: To change Readiness Status, click on Subsystem settings.
