PDM

Predictive Data Migration (PDM) is the migration of data from the suspect disk drive to a spare disk drive, similar to Rebuilding a Logical Drive. But unlike Rebuilding, PDM constantly monitors your disk drives and automatically copies your data to a spare disk drive BEFORE the disk drive fails and your Logical Drive goes Critical.

The following actions trigger PDM:

  • A disk drive with unhealthy status (see below)
  • Media Patrol finds a disk critical error*
  • You initiate PDM manually

*PDM also counts the number of media errors reported by Media Patrol.

A disk drive becomes unhealthy when:

  • A SMART error is reported

  • The bad sector remapping table fills to the specified level.

Because data would be lost if written to a bad sector, when a bad sector is detected, the disk drive creates a map around it. These maps are saved in the bad sector remapping table, which has a capacity of 512 reassigned blocks and 1024 error blocks.

You can specify a percentage level for the reassigned and error blocks in Background Activities.

When the table fills to a specified percentage of its capacity, PDM triggers a migration of data from the suspect drive (the disk drive with the bad sectors) to a spare disk drive.

During data migration, you will have access to the Logical Drive but it will respond more slowly to read/write tasks because of the additional operation. The time required for data migration depends on the size of the disk drive.

To run PDM, take the following actions:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. From the dropdown menu on the Background Activities tab, select Start PDM.
  3. From the Source Physical Drive dropdown menu, select a Source disk array and physical drive.
    Arrays have a number. Physical drives have Seq. No. and a number.
  4. From the Target Physical Drive dropdown menu, select a Target physical drive.
  5. Click on the Submit button.

A PDM link appears on the Disk Array Background Activities tab and the percent completed displays.

Pause, Resume, or Stop PDM

To pause, resume, or stop the Reuild:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Disk Arrays icon.
  3. Click on the Disk Array icon.
  4. Click on the Background Activities tab in Management view.
  5. Under Background Activities, click on the PDM link.

Note: You can also pause, resume, or stop PDM from the Subsystem icon Background Activities tab.

Set PDM Priority

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. From the dropdown menu on the Background Activities tab, select Settings.
  3. Click on the PDM Rate dropdown menu and select a rate: Low, Medium or High.
    • Low allocates fewer system resources to the PDM and more to data read/write operations.
    • Medium allocates a balance of system resources to the PDM and data read/write operations.
    • High allocates more system resources to the PDM and fewer to data read/write operations.
  4. Click on the Submit button.
