Storage Network

The Storage Network consists of all the subsystem enclosures currently accessable on your network. When you log into a , displays only that subsystem in the browser window.

To view the other subsystems on your Storage Network:

  1. Click on the Storage Network in the Header.
  2. Click on the Show Network Subsystems popup option.

will display the Network Subsystems in the Tree.
You must click on the Subsystems icon in the Tree to see the other subsystems.
Networked subsytems are identified in the Tree by their Management Port IP addresses.

To hide the Storage Network:

Click Storage Network again to hide the Network Subsystems.

In order to minimize network traffic, does not automatically update the list when Strorage Network is enabled.

To update the subsystem list:

  1. Click on the Subsytems icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Discover button in Management View.
