SCSI Channel Settings

The SCSI Management–Channels tab enables you to make Data Channel settings, including:

  • Channel ID – 1 or 2
  • Target ID List – Target ID scope is from 0 to 15
  • Termination – Automatic, On or Off

To make SCSI channel settings:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree view.
  2. Click on the Administration Tools icon.
  3. Click on the SCSI Management icon.
  4. Click the Channel tab in Management View.
  5. Click the Channel 1 or Channel 2 link.
  6. In the Target ID list, check a TID box to enable the Target ID. Uncheck the box to disable it.
  7. From the Termination dropdown menu, select the one of the following:
    • Automatic – Termination is managed automatically (default)
    • On – Termination is on
    • Off – Termination is off
  8. Click the Submit button.
    Termination settings take effect immediately.
  9. If you changed the Target ID enable/disable settings, restart the .

Caution: Turn off termination only when the is NOT the last device in the SCSI chain.
