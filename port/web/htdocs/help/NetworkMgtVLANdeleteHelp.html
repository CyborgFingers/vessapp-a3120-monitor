Delete a VLAN

The Network Management–VLAN tab enables you to delete a virtual local area network (VLAN).

To delete a VLAN entry:

  1. Click the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click the Administrative Tools icon.
  3. Click the Network Management icon.
  4. In Management View, from the dropdown menu on the VLAN tab, choose Delete VLAN Entry.
  5. Check the box to the left of the VLAN entry you want to delete.
  6. Click the Submit button.
