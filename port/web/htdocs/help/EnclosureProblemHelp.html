Enclosure Problem

When a yellow exclamation point (!) or a red X appears over an Enclosure icon in Tree View, click on the icon. The Enclosure screen will display in Management View.

Overheating

A flashing red thermometer icon indicates Overheating. Overheating is a potentially serious condition because the excessively high temperatures can lead to disk drive failure and controller malfunction.

Overheating usually results from:

  • Fan or blower failure (see Fans / Blowers below)

  • Poor air circulation around the enclosure

Air circulation around the enclosure may be a more complex problem. Use the thermometer icons to help you locate the specific hot spot. Check for these conditions:

  • Accumulated dust or objects blocking the fans

  • Less than a minimum of 5 inches (13 cm) space between the back of the and the wall or other object

  • Ambient temperature above 95°F (35°C) where the is operating

To cool down a , take the following actions:

  • Correct any problems identified above

  • Power it down and let it sit for an hour or longer

Fans / Blowers

subsystems use a combination of fans and blowers for cooling:

  • Power supplies, 1 fan each, 2 fans total

  • Cooling unit, 2 blowers

A fan or blower with red blades that do not turn indicates a failure. If a power supply fan fails, you must replace the power supply. If a cooling unit blower fails, you can remove the cooling unit and replace only the blower itself.

No tools are required for either procedure. See Chapter 6 of the Product Manual for instructions on replacing the fans.
