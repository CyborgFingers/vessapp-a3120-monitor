User Management

The User Management–Information tab lists the users, their status, access privileges, display name, and email address. To access this tab:

  1. Click the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click the Administrative Tools icon.
  3. Click on the User Management icon.

To add a user, log in as the Admistrator or a Super User, then click the Create tab in Management View.

To delete a user, log in as the Admistrator or a Super User, then click the Delete tab in Management View.

To access user settings, log in as the Admistrator or a Super User, then click on a name link in the User list.

Users can access their own settings and passwords.
