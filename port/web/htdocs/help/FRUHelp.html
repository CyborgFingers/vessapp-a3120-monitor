FRU

Field Replaceable Unit (FRU) refers to the components that you, the user, can replace in the field. They are:

  • Power Supplies
  • Cooling Unit(s)
  • RAID Controller
  • Storage Enclosure Processor (SEP)

Refer to Chapter 6 of the Product Manual for replacement instructions and Chapter 8 for Troubleshooting procedures.
