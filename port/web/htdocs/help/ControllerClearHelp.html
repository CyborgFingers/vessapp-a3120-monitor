Controller Clear Condition

The Controller–Clear tab enables you to clear an Orphan Watermark condition.
The Clear tab only appears when this condition is present.

An Orphan Watermark condition is the result of a disk drive failure during an NVRAM RAID level migration on a disk array.

To clear an Orphan Watermark:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Controllers icon.
  3. Click on the Controller 1 or Controller 2 icon.
  4. Click on the Clear tab in Management View.
  5. Click the Submit button.
    The changes take effect immediately.

Note: If your subsystem has two controllers, clearing a condition on one controller will automatically apply to the other controller.
