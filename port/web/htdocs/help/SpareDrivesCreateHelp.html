Create a Spare Drive

The Spare Drives–Create tab enables you to create new spare drives.

Note: Be sure the spare drive you select has adequate capacity to replace the largest physical drive in your disk arrays.

To create a spare drive:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Spare Drives icon.
  3. Click on the Create tab in Management View.
  4. Select a spare type, Global or Dedicated.
    Global can be used by any disk array. Dedicated can only be used by the assigned disk array
  5. To make a revertible spare drive, check the Revertible box.
    A revertible spare drive automatically returns to its spare drive assignment after the failed physical drive in the disk array is replaced. See Transition.
  6. Highlight physical drives you want in as a spare drive the Available list and press the >> button to move them to the Selected list.
    You can also double-click them to move them.
  7. If you selected a Dedicated spare drive, highlight disk array to which you want assign the spare drive from the Available list and press the >> button to move them to the Selected list.
    You can also double-click them to move them.
    You can dedicate one spare drive to multiple disk arrays.
  8. Click the Update button.
    Your choices are displayed under New Hot Spare Drives.
  9. If you agree with the proposed choices, click the Submit button.
    The new spare drive is added to the Spare Drive List on the Information tab.
