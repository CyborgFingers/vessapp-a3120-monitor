Enclosure Information

The Enclosure–Information tab provides status information about the subsystem enclosure, including:

  • An enclosure diagram showing the status and location of key components.
  • Enclosure Type
  • Enclosure Warning and Critical temperature thresholds*
  • Controller Warning and Critical temperature thresholds*
  • Maximum number of Controllers, Physical Drives, Fans, Blowers, Temperature Sensors, Power Supply Units, and Voltage Sensors in the enclosure
  • Power Supply Units – Device ID and Operational Status
  • Fans (scroll fans in the power supply unit(s) – Device ID, Location, Operational Status, and Speed
  • Blowers (scroll fans in the cooling unit(s) – Device ID, Location, Operational Status, and Speed
  • Temperature Sensors – The number of sensors varies among models
  • Voltage Sensors – 1.1V or 1.2V; 3.3V, 5.0V, and 12.0V. The number of sensors varies among models

You can make setting adjustments to the items marked with an asterisk (*). Click the Settings tab for access.

See Enclosure Problem for information about how malfunctions are reported and managed.
