Delete a User

The User Management–Delete tab enables the Administrator or a Super User to delete other users. To delete a user:

  1. Click the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click the Administration Tools icon.
  3. Click on the User Management icon.
  4. Click on the Delete tab in Management View.
  5. Check the box to the left of the user you want to delete.
  6. Click the Submit button.
  7. Click OK in the confirmation box.

Note: There will always be at least one Super User or Administrator acount.

Note: You cannot delete your own account.
