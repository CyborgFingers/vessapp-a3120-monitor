Physical Drive Force Offline/Online

The Physical Drive–Force Offline/Online tab provides enables you to force an:

  • Online physical drive to go Offline
  • Offline physical drive to go Online

Warning: Forcing a physical drive offline or online is likely to cause data loss. Back up your data before you proceed. Use these functions only when required.

Important: Forcing a physical drive offline will cause your logical drives to become degraded. If Auto Rebuild is enabled and a spare drive is available, the disk array will begin rebuilding itself automatically.

This tab appears only when you select a physical drive that belongs to a Disk Array.

To force a physical drive offline or online:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Enclosures icon.
  3. Click on the Enclosure icon.
  4. Click on the Physical Drives icon.
  5. Click on a Physical Drive icon.
  6. Click on the Force Offline/Online tab in Management View.
  7. Click the Submit button.
  8. In the confirmation box, type the word confirm in the field provided.
  9. Click the OK button.
