Subsystem Background Activities

The Subsystem–Background Activities tab provides information about functions running in the background on your subsystem. Click on the link below for information about the settings and options available for each feature.

Background activities include:

  • Disk array Rebuilding
  • Disk array Synchronization
  • Logical Drive Initialization
  • Disk array Migration
  • Disk array Transition
  • Logical Drive Redundancy Check
  • Predictive Data Migration (PDM)
  • Media Patrol

To view the background activities currently running on your subsystem:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click the Background Activities tab in Management View.
    Any currently running activities are listed under Background Activities.
  3. Click on the link for the activity you want to monitor.

To pause an activity, click the Pause button. To resume again, click the Resume button.

To cancel an activity, click the Stop button.

To start an activity, from the dropdown menu on the Background Activities tab, click on the activity you want to start.
