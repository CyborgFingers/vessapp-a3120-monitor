Troubleshooting

aids in troubleshooting your disk arrays, logical drives and enclosure by continuous monitoring and reporting to you in the following ways:

  • Sounds an audible alarm to inform you that the needs attention.
  • Displays yellow exclamation points (!) and red Xs in Tree View.
  • Sends an email notification (see Event Subscription).
  • Keeps a record in the Event Log (above).
  • Displays full information on the selected component the Management Window.
