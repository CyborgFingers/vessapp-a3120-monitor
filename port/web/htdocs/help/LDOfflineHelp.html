Logical Drive Offline

When a logical drive goes Offline, the data stored in the logical drive is no longer accessible.
Logical drives based on fault-tolerant disk arrays—RAID 1, 1E, 5, 6, and 10—go Offline when two physical drives are removed or fail.
Logical drives based on non-fault tolerant disk arrays—RAID 0—go Offline when a one physical drive is removed or fails.

RAID 1, 1E, 5, 6, and 10 Logical Drives

If a fault-tolerant logical drive—RAID 1, 1E, 5, 6, and 10—goes offline, it may be possible to recover your data.

Warning: Take no further corrective action until you have consulted with Technical Support!

RAID 0 Logical Drives

If a logical drive based on a non-fault-tolerant disk array—RAID 0—goes offline, all of the data on the logical drive is lost.
Do the following to recreate your logical drive:

  1. Identify the failed physical drive.
  2. Replace the failed drive.
  3. If the disk array had more than one physical drive, delete the disk array and re-create it.
  4. Restore the data from your backup source.

Important: If your replacement disk drive was formerly part of a different disk array or logical drive, you must wipe the reserve sector on the replacement disk drive before you use it. Use the Command Line Interface (CLI). See Chapter 5 of the Product Manual for instructions.

For further information, see Troubleshooting.
