Adding a Second Controller

If your subsystem shipped with one controller, you can add a second controller. The second controller must have:

  • The same Firmware version as the currently installed controller
  • The same amount of SDRAM as the currently installed controller

To obtain this information for the currently installed controller, click on the Controller icon, Information tab, and look for Firmware Version and Memory Size.

Note: Obtain your second controller though . Support will prepare the new controller with Firmware and SDRAM to match your current subsystem.

Note: The subsystem boots its controllers sequentially. With a second controller installed, your subsystem will take about a minute longer to boot. This condition is normal.

Installing a Second Controller

To install a second controller in your subsystem:

  1. Be sure the new controller has the same Firmware version and amount of SDRAM.
  2. Shutdown the subsystem.
  3. Remove the blank cover from the right controller slot.
  4. Carefully slide the new controller into the slot until the handle locks in place.
  5. Attach your data and management cables to the new controller, as needed.
    See the Product Manual for cable connection information.
  6. Power up the subsystem and launch WebPAM PROe.
  7. Click on the Controllers icon in the Tree, then look at the Information tab in Management view.
    • If the controllers' Operational Status is OK, the installation was successful.
    • If one of the controller's Operational Status is Missing, one of the controllers went into maintenance mode.
  8. With the second controller successfully installed, make the following settings:
    • Redundancy Type to Active-Active or Active-Standby
    • LUN Affinity if you choose Active-Active redundancy

Dual Controllers and SATA Drives

If your subsystem has SATA disk drives installed, you must install an AAMUX adapter on each of the SATA drives.
Without the AAMUX adapter, SATA drives will display Not Usable under Operational Status.
See the Product Manual for disk drive and AAMUX installation instructions.

This condition does not apply to SAS disk drives.

Note: Obtain AAMUX adapters though .
