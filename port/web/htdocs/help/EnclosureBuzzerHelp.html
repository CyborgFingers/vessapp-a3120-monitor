Enclosure Buzzer

The Enclosure–Buzzer tab enables you to change the audible alarm (buzzer) settings for the subsystem enclosure.

When you first power-up the , the buzzer beeps twice to show normal operation.

The buzzer sounds at other times to inform you that the needs attention.
But the buzzer does not specify the condition.
When the buzzer sounds, take the following actions:

  • Check the front and back of the subsystem for red or amber LEDs.
  • If email notification is enabled, check for new messages.
  • Check for yellow !s red Xs in Tree View.
  • Check the Event Log.

When a continuous tone sounds, there are multiple alarm patterns sounding at the same time.
See Troubleshooting for more information.

Silence Buzzer

You can silence the buzzer for the current trigger event.
To silence the Buzzer, take the following actions:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Enclosures icon.
  3. Click on the Enclosure icon.
  4. Click on the Buzzer tab in Management View.
  5. Click the Mute button.
    The buzzer goes silent. If another trigger event occurs, the buzzer will sound again.

Enable or Disable Buzzer

To change Buzzer settings, take the following actions:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Enclosures icon.
  3. Click on the Enclosure icon.
  4. In Management View, from the dropdown menu on the Buzzer tab, select Settings.
  5. To enable the buzzer, check the Enable Buzzer box.
    Uncheck the box to disable or silence the buzzer.
  6. Click the Submit button.
    The setting changes immediately.

Test Buzzer

You must enable buzzer before you can test it.
To test the Buzzer, do the following actions:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Enclosures icon.
  3. Click on the Enclosure icon.
  4. Click on the Buzzer tab in Management View.
  5. Click the Sound button.
    The buzzer sounds.
  6. Click the Mute button to silence the buzzer.

Also see Troubleshooting.
