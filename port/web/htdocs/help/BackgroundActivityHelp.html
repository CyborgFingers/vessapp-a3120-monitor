Background Activity Progress

The Background Activities tab provides information about background activities running and queued on your subsystem, disk array, or logical drive. For most background activities, you can pause, resume, or cancel the activity from this screen.

Subsystem Background Activity:

  • Media Patrol

Logical Drive Background Activities:

  • Initialization
  • Redundancy Check

Disk Array Background Activities:

  • Disk array Migration
  • Disk array Rebuild
  • Predictive Data Migration (PDM)
  • Spare drive Transition

Monitoring Activity

Each background activity has its own set of parameters. The most important parameters are:

  • Status
    • Running – Now in progress.
    • Paused – Waiting for a higher priority activity to finish OR waiting for you to click the Resume button.
  • Progress

Pause, Resume, Stop

To pause a background activity, click the Pause button.
To resume again, click the Resume button.

To stop (cancel) a background activity, click the Stop button.
If a confirmation box displays, type the word confirm in the field provided, then click the OK button.

Note: Disk array migration does not allow you to pause, resume, or stop the activity once it begins.

Viewing Background Activity Progress

To view the progress of the current background activity and a list of all activities queued within the subsystem:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click the Background Activities tab in Management view.
  3. Under the Background Activities list, click on the link for the activity you want to monitor.

To view the progress of the current Disk Array background activity and a list of activities in the Disk Array queue:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Disk Arrays icon.
  3. Click on the Disk Array icon.
  4. Click the Background Activities tab in Management view.
  5. Under the Background Activities list, click on the link for the activity you want to monitor.

To view the progress of the current Logical Drive background activity and a list of activities in the Logical Drive queue:

  1. Click on the Subsystem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Disk Arrays icon.
  3. Click on the Disk Array icon.
  4. Click on the Logical Drives icon.
  5. Click on the Logical Drive icon.
  6. Click the Background Activities tab in Management view.
  7. Under the Background Activities list, click on the link for the activity you want to monitor.
