Enclosure FRU VPD

The Enclosure–FRU VPD tab displays Vital Product Data (VPD) information about Field Replaceable Units (FRU) in the subsystem enclosure. The number and type of FRU depends on the subystem model.

  • Controllers
  • Storage Enclosure Processor (SEP)
  • Battery Backup Unit (BBU)
  • Blowers or Cooling Units
  • Power Supply Units
  • Back Plane
  • Daughter Card (attaches to the Controller)

Use this information when communicating with Technical Support and when ordering replacement parts.
