Physical Drive Settings

The Physical Drive–Settings tab enables you to specify an alias for a configured physical disk drive.

To make physical drive settings:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Enclosures icon.
  3. Click on the Enclosure icon.
  4. Click on the Physical Drives icon.
  5. Click on the Physical Drive icon.
  6. Click on the Settings tab in Management View.
  7. Enter a name into the Alias field.
    Maximum of 32 characters. Use letters, numbers, space between words and underscore.
  8. Click the Submit button.

Note: All other physical drive settings are located on the Physical Drives Global Settings tab.
