Physical Drive Problems

When a yellow exclamation point (!) or a red X appears over an Physical Drive icon in Tree View, click on the icon.
The Enclosure screen will display in Management View. Look under Operational Status for the condition of the physical drive.

  • Offline – Check the drive for:
    • PFA Condition – Caused by a bad block or sector. See Note 1 below
    • Stale Configuration – Caused by obsolete array information on the physical drive. See Note 2 below
  • Drive Failed – The physical drive cannot be repaired. You must replace the failed drive.

Note 1: Clear the error condition. The physical drive will then be available.

Note 2: Identify the disk array to which the physical drive belongs. Then delete the disk array.
If the error condition remains on the physical drive, clear the error condition.

See also: Media Patrol, PDM, and Logical Drive Degraded.
