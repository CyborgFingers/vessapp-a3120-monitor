Enclosure Settings

The Enclosure–Settings tab enables you to make temperature threshold settings for the subsystem enclosure.

To make Enclosure settings:

  1. Click on the Subsytem icon in Tree View.
  2. Click on the Enclosures icon.
  3. Click on the Enclosure icon.
  4. Click on the Settings tab in Management View.
  5. Enter a new value in the following fields as necessary.
    The range for each setting is shown in °C:
    • Controller Warning Temperature Threshold
    • Controller Critical Temperature Threshold
  6. To enable Power Switch, check the Power Switch box.
  7. Click the Submit button.
    The changes take effect immediately.

See Enclosure Problem for information about how malfunctions are reported and managed.
